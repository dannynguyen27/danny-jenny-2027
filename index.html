Frequently Asked Questions

When should I arrive?

Please arrive promptly by 4:00 PM to allow time for valet parking and seating before the ceremony begins at 4:30 PM.

Where is the wedding?

The ceremony and reception will be held at Bel Air Bay Club, 16801 Pacific Coast Highway, Pacific Palisades, CA 90272.

Open in Maps ↗

What should I wear?

Formal attire is requested; black tie is optional. The ceremony will be outdoors overlooking the ocean, so we recommend bringing a light layer for the evening breeze.

Is parking available?

Yes. Complimentary valet parking will be available at the venue.

Are children invited?

We are planning an adults-only celebration. Any exceptions will be noted on your invitation.

Can I bring a guest?

Your RSVP will indicate everyone included in your party. If a plus-one has been reserved for you, their name can be added when you RSVP.

How and when should I RSVP?

Please use the RSVP link above to respond by December 31, 2026. We kindly ask each invited guest to include their meal and dietary information when responding.

Will the celebration be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will take place outdoors on the Ocean Lawn. Please plan for coastal weather and check back closer to the wedding for any updated details.

Who should I contact with additional questions?

Please email dannynhi827@gmail.com, and we will be happy to help.

How it began

Our Story

Our story began in 2003, when we became friends as next-door neighbors. We spent our early years learning to ride bikes together, swimming through countless summers, and sharing the everyday adventures that shaped our childhood. As we grew older, life took us in different directions, and we spent several years apart. Yet no matter how much time or distance came between us, that bond stayed quietly in the background. In 2023, we found our way back to one another and reconnected as though no time had passed. The friendship that began all those years ago had never truly left us, and what started as a childhood bond became a lifelong promise to each other.

Danny & Jenny
